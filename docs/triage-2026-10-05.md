# Triage of the open items, 5 October

The owner asked, on 1 October, for a triage before any new fix: reproduce every open item on a lane and drop what
does not reproduce; move features, ClickUp gaps and decisions to a separate list that does not count as open
defects; and from then on count a finding only once it is reproduced.

How it was done: 69 of the 77 open and partly fixed items, in 14 groups of related items, one agent per group
on its own lane (1, 3, 4, 6 or 7) at `2a53729`. Each agent read the item, classified it, and reproduced each
defect on the running app (API probes, a browser, the gate concerned on crafted input). Then a skeptic per group,
on another lane, tried to prove wrong every verdict that was not "reproduced", by another route where it could.
The other 8 items were found while closing the three changes the owner had reverted, and are triaged after the revert.

**Outcome.** No item had to be dropped: every defect and tooling item reproduced. 22 stay counted (their defect
reproduced, some only in part); 47 are features, decisions or deploy settings and now sit in the ledger's
uncounted section. The skeptics found 7 reproduced defects inside items that had been called features; each is
a new counted item (`found-5-oct/*`).

Every entry below is what the agent returned, with the skeptic's answer where there was one. Paths under
`/tmp/claude-0/triage/` are scratch files on the machine that ran it.
