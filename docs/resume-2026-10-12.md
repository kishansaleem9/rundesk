## 6. Working rules worth repeating

- Close each item as ClickUp works it: research ClickUp (through web search;
  the help centre is blocked here), build it end to end, and measure before
  and after on a lane (the lanes are localhost). Then break-test every check,
  register the suite in `ci.yml`, run the preflight and a regression, write
  the ledger, and commit.
- Push only what a rehearsal passed (`bash scripts/ci-rehearsal/all.sh`).
  `ALLOW_SCHEMA_RESET` stays unset on production deploys.
- Postgres here allows 100 connections: sweep on four lanes at most and stop
  the APIs of lanes not in use. A suite's `fill` has no timeout of its own;
  set the page's. A suite closes its stack with `stack.close()`, and a step
  that can fail in the browser fails its check, not the run.
- No subagents or workflows: the owner asked for the work to be done directly,
  for cost.
- Commit messages end with the session trailer, and nothing pushed names a
  model.
